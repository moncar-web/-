# 썸네일 이미지 프롬프트 — 헌 솜이불 한 채

공통 설정: Seed 고정 / 16:9 (1280x720) / 인물 중심축 우측 30% 이동 / 좌측은 넓은 풍경(제목 자막 공간)

## A안 (메인 추천) — 이불을 가르는 순간
Korean traditional art blended with modern webtoon style, Joseon Dynasty 1700s-1800s, high quality, maximum detail, mystical, warm and dramatic mood.
16:9 composition. Characters' central axis shifted 30% to the right side of the frame; the left side shows a wide landscape.
Right side: a young Joseon bride, 17 years old, in a plain white cotton jeogori, hair in a married woman's bun with a simple wooden binyeo, kneeling on the wooden floor of a humble thatched house. With trembling hands she cuts open the seam of an old faded, patched cotton quilt with scissors; a stream of red-awned rice seeds and golden husks pours out of the quilt like a waterfall, glowing softly in candlelight. Tears on her cheeks, eyes wide with shock and realization. A folded letter wrapped in oiled paper tied with thread lies on the quilt.
Behind her, a stern middle-aged mother-in-law and a teenage sister-in-law peeking in, frozen in surprise.
Left side: through an open paper door, a wide view of a flooded riverside village at dawn, muddy water, a broken earthen embankment, willow trees budding with spring green, mist over the river.
Warm amber candlelight on the right contrasting with cool blue-gray dawn on the left, floating dust and rice husks in the light rays, cinematic lighting, sharp focus on the seeds and the bride's face.
No text, no letters, no watermark.

## B안 — 혼례 전날 새벽, 비단 대신 헌 이불
Korean traditional art blended with modern webtoon style, Joseon Dynasty 1700s-1800s, high quality, maximum detail, mystical, warm and dramatic mood.
16:9 composition. Characters' central axis shifted 30% to the right; the left side shows a wide landscape.
Right side: on the narrow wooden porch (maru) of a small mountain cottage, a stern widowed seamstress mother in her fifties, weathered face, needle-pricked fingertips, thimble on her finger, pushes an old stained, patched cotton quilt toward her 17-year-old daughter. The daughter, in a plain cotton hanbok, stands shocked with tears, one hand on an empty open wall cabinet where silk wedding garments used to be. A small oil lamp, bundles of boiled mugwort drying on the eaves, a faint green steam of mugwort.
Left side: a wide misty mountain valley before dawn, terraced rice paddies, a winding stream and a footbridge, the last stars fading.
Contrast between the warm lamp glow and the deep indigo pre-dawn sky, emotional, dramatic.
No text, no letters, no watermark.

## C안 — 관아 마당, 붉은 볍씨의 증거
Korean traditional art blended with modern webtoon style, Joseon Dynasty 1700s-1800s, high quality, maximum detail, mystical, warm and dramatic mood.
16:9 composition. Characters' central axis shifted 30% to the right; the left side shows a wide landscape.
Right side: in a Joseon government office courtyard, a young bride with loosened rope marks on her wrists kneels upright with a determined face, pointing to a small wooden tray holding two heaps of rice seeds, one with red awns and one with white awns. Beside her an old hunched ferryman with a walking stick testifies. In the background a fat landlord in a silk dopo and gat, sweating, collapsing to his knees.
Left side: wide view over the courtyard wall of crowded villagers and a river with a ferry boat, golden afternoon light, willow trees.
Dramatic sunlight beams, tense but cathartic atmosphere.
No text, no letters, no watermark.
